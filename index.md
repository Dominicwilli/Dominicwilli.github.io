I am a Programme Officer at the [Centre for Development Research Enlightenment and Advocacy (CDREA)](https://cdreang.github.io/Cdrea/) and a Research Assistant at the [University of Uyo](https://uniuyo.edu.ng), where I work under Professor Etim Frank on administrative capacity and policy implementation in Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. My research interests are public policy, public administration, and how policy is actually implemented at the subnational level. I am also the founder of [EduGovernance Hub Nigeria](https://web.facebook.com/groups/502743330660504), a governance and civic education platform with over 50,000 members.
