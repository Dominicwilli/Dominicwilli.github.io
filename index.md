## Research

Williams Edidiong Dominic is a Programme Officer at the Centre for Development Research Enlightenment and Advocacy (CDREA) and a Research Assistant in the Department of Political Science and Public Administration at the University of Uyo, Nigeria, where he works under Professor Etim Frank. His work sits at the intersection of public policy, public administration, and administrative capacity, with a particular focus on how policy is actually implemented at the subnational level in Nigeria.

At CDREA, he coordinates governance and civic engagement programmes funded by UNDEF across rural communities in Akwa Ibom State, combining research with direct advocacy and stakeholder engagement. His academic research, developed alongside Professor Frank, examines the role of the civil service and administrative capacity in policy execution in Akwa Ibom State between 2015 and 2025.

He holds a BSc in Political Science and Public Administration from the University of Uyo, graduating in the top 10 percent of his cohort. He is also the founder of EduGovernance Hub Nigeria, a youth-focused governance and civic education platform with over 50,000 members, and a member of the 2026 Aspire Leaders Program cohort, run in collaboration with Harvard Business School.

### Research Interests

- Public policy and public administration
- Administrative capacity
- Policy implementation, particularly at the subnational level in Nigeria
