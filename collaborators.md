**Professor Etim Frank**
Department of Public Administration, University of Uyo

Professor Frank is Williams's research supervisor and long-time academic collaborator. Their joint work spans a co-authored book, *Social and Political Philosophy: The Global Perspective* (2026), and two journal articles on administrative capacity and civil service policy execution in Akwa Ibom State. Professor Frank also supervised Williams's undergraduate dissertation on the role of civil service in public policy implementation.
